## 1. Logging In

### First time using the device?
1. Open the app — you will see a **Select Your Device** screen.
2. Find your device name in the list and tap it.
3. Confirm your **branch** and **area**, then tap **Claim Device**.
4. You will be taken to the **Login** screen.

### Every day login
1. Enter your **email** and **password**.
2. Tap **Login**.

> **No internet?** Tap **Offline Mode** instead. You must have logged in online at least once before for this to work.

### Trouble logging in?
| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This device is not yet assigned to a branch and area." | Ask your supervisor to assign the device first. |
| "Incorrect password." | Double-check your password and try again. |
| "Need internet for online login." | Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data, then try again. |

## 2. Opening Your Shift (Open Cash)

You must open cash **before** accepting any transactions.

1. After logging in, you will land on the **Open Cash** screen automatically.
2. Count your **opening float** (the cash you start with) and enter the amount using the keypad.
3. Add a **note** if needed (e.g. "Received balance from supervisor").
4. Tap **Open Cash and Start Shift**.

### Inherited tickets
If the previous cashier left vehicles still parked, a list will appear titled **"Transactions From Previous Shift."** These tickets will be transferred to your shift.

- Tap **Acknowledge & Continue** to accept them and open your shift.
- Tap **Cancel** if you need to adjust your opening balance first.

## 3. Checking In a Vehicle

From the **Dashboard**, tap **Check In Vehicle**. The app will reserve a ticket number and walk you through 6 steps.

### Step 1 — Customer & Valet
- Enter the **customer's name** and **contact number**.
- Select **Standard Valet** or **Self Park**.
- Assign a **driver**, confirm the **date/time in**, and add any **special instructions** if needed.

### Step 2 — Vehicle Details
- Enter the **plate number**, **brand**, **color**, and **vehicle type** (sedan, SUV, etc.).
- Select the **parking level** and **slot** where the vehicle will be parked.
  > The parking slot is required — you cannot continue without selecting one.

### Step 3 — Personal Belongings
- Tick any items the customer is leaving in the vehicle (iPad, phone/charger, laptop, sunglasses, other).
- Leave blank if the customer has nothing to declare.

### Step 4 — Vehicle Condition
- Tap the vehicle diagram to mark any **existing damage** (dents, scratches, etc.).
- Have the customer **sign on the screen** to confirm the vehicle's condition.
  > The customer's signature is required to proceed.

### Step 5 — Review & Confirm
- Check that all details are correct.
- Tap **Confirm** to save the check-in.

### Step 6 — Print Ticket
- The app will print a **3-part ticket** to your Bluetooth printer.
- Print **Part 1**, then **Part 2**, then **Part 3** in order.
- You can reprint any part if the paper jams.
- When all parts are printed, tap **Finish** to return to the Dashboard.

## 4. Checking Out a Vehicle

From the **Dashboard**, tap **Checkout Vehicle**. The app will guide you through 5 steps.

### Step 1 — Find the Ticket
- **Scan the QR code** on the customer's ticket stub using the camera, **or**
- Type the **ticket number** or **plate number** manually and tap **Search**.

### Step 2 — Vehicle Info
- Review the vehicle details, parking duration, and estimated fee.
- Switch to the **Condition Check** tab to compare the vehicle's condition from check-in.

### Step 3 — Condition Check
- Compare any damage from check-in vs. now.
- Mark any **new damage** if present.

### Step 4 — Payment
- Review the **fee breakdown** (parking duration, any overnight rates).
- **Lost ticket?** Toggle **"Lost ticket (no stub)"** — this adds the lost-ticket fee. Internet is required for this.
- Enter the **name of the driver** returning the vehicle.
- Enter the **cash tendered** — the app will calculate the **change** for you.
- Tap **Confirm Payment**.

### Step 5 — Payment Complete
- The screen shows the total, amount tendered, and change.
- Tap **Print Via Bluetooth** to print the customer's receipt.
- Tap **Release Vehicle / Done** to return to the Dashboard.

## 5. Manual Ticketing (Express Cashier)

If your role is **Express Cashier**, you will see a **Manual Ticketing** screen instead of the full Dashboard. This is a simplified mode for recording payments quickly.

### Saving a ticket
1. Enter the **plate number**.
2. Enter the **ticket number** (e.g. `TKT-2024-0087`).
3. Enter the **amount** (in pesos).
4. Optionally enter the **VR number**.
5. Tap **Save**.

A confirmation will appear: *"Saved [ticket ID]."*

### Transaction list
The right side of the screen shows all transactions for your current shift. Rows with a **cloud-off icon** have not yet synced to the server — this is normal when working offline or on a slow connection.

### Clearing the form
Tap **Cancel** at the bottom to clear all fields without saving.

## 6. Printing Receipts

All printing is done via a **Bluetooth thermal printer**.

### Setting up your printer (first time)
1. Go to **Settings** (gear icon in the side menu).
2. Tap **Bluetooth Printer**.
3. Follow the on-screen steps to pair your printer.

### What gets printed and when
| Action | What is printed |
|---|---|
| Check-in complete | 3-part check-in ticket |
| Checkout payment confirmed | Customer payment receipt |
| Shift closed (standard cashier) | End-of-shift summary receipt |
| Settings → Test Print | Test page |

### If printing fails
- Make sure the printer is **on** and **Bluetooth is enabled** on your device.
- Go to **Settings → Bluetooth Printer** and tap **Reconnect**.
- Try the print again.

## 7. Closing Your Shift (Close Cash)

You must close cash at the end of every shift.

1. Tap the **logout icon** (or go to the side menu and tap **Logout**).
2. Choose **Close Cash + Logout**.
3. Enter your **password** to confirm.
4. The **Close Cash** screen will open.

### Before you can close
The app will check the following — all must be clear:
- You are **connected to the internet**.
- **All transactions are synced** (no pending or failed items).

If any of these are not met, follow the on-screen message and resolve them first (see [Settings & Sync](#9-settings--sync)).

### Counting your cash
- Count the total cash you are turning in and enter it using the keypad.
- The screen shows your shift statistics (check-ins, checkouts, revenue) for reference.
- If vehicles are still parked, you will see a warning — inform the next cashier or supervisor.

### Finishing up
- Tap **Close Cash**.
- A **shift summary receipt** will print automatically (standard cashier only).
- You will be logged out and returned to the Login screen.

## 8. Working Offline

The app can work **without internet**, with a few limitations.

| Action | Works offline? |
|---|---|
| Login (Offline Mode) | Yes — if you logged in online at least once before |
| Check in a vehicle | Yes — saved locally and synced when back online |
| Check out a vehicle | Yes — except **lost ticket checkout** requires internet |
| Manual ticketing (express) | Yes — synced when back online |
| Close cash | **No** — must be online and fully synced |

### How to tell if you're offline
Look for the **Offline** pill at the top of the screen (Open Cash, Close Cash, or Manual Ticketing headers). Reports will also show a banner: *"You're offline — showing local records only."*

### Cloud-off icons
Rows with a **cloud-off icon** in any list mean that transaction has not synced yet. It will sync automatically once you are back online.

## 9. Settings & Sync

Access **Settings** from the side menu (gear icon).

### Syncing transactions
- **Pending count** shows how many items are waiting to upload.
- Tap **Sync Now** to manually force a sync.
- **Auto-sync** is on by default — the app will sync automatically when you reconnect to the internet.

### Bluetooth printer
- **Reconnect** — reconnect to the saved printer.
- **Test Print** — print a test page to verify the connection.

### Other settings
- View your **profile** (name, role, branch).
- Toggle **light or dark theme**.
- **Logout** from here or from the side menu.

## 10. Common Questions

**Q: I can't log in and it says my device isn't assigned.**
A: Ask your supervisor to assign this device to a branch and area in the management portal first.

**Q: I accidentally clicked Cancel on the inherited tickets screen — what do I do?**
A: Adjust your opening balance if needed, then tap **Open Cash and Start Shift** again. The inherited tickets prompt will appear once more.

**Q: The customer lost their ticket. Can I still check them out?**
A: Yes. On the Payment step, toggle **"Lost ticket (no stub)"**. This will add the lost-ticket fee. Note: you must be online to use this option.

**Q: The printer is not responding.**
A: Go to **Settings → Bluetooth Printer** and tap **Reconnect**. Make sure the printer is powered on and Bluetooth is enabled on your device.

**Q: I can't close cash — it says there are pending items.**
A: Go to **Settings → Sync Now**. Wait for it to complete and check that the pending count reaches zero. Then try closing cash again.

**Q: I see cloud-off icons next to some transactions. Is that a problem?**
A: Not immediately — it just means those transactions haven't uploaded yet. They will sync automatically once you have internet. Do not close cash until all cloud-off icons are gone.

**Q: I'm an express cashier — can I do check-ins or check-outs?**
A: No. Express cashiers only use the Manual Ticketing screen. For full valet operations, contact your supervisor.
